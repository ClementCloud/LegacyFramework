Year by year

Click a row to jump to that year.

How the math works: Today shows balances as entered, and each later month shows balances at the end of that month. Each income uses one combined effective tax rate. Pay and spending rise once a year by the rates you set. Mortgages amortize monthly. Cash above the reserve target moves to the investment account when that setting is on.
Savings rate counts what's left over plus 401(k) contributions and match, out of take-home pay plus those contributions. Leave pay uses the benefit rate and weekly cap set under Life events. The child tax credit default is the 2026 federal amount ($2,200 per child), spread across the year here, though it actually arrives with the tax refund.
This is a planning model, not tax or financial advice.
